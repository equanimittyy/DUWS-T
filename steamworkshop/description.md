[h1][b]DUWS_T (Dynamic Universal War System Tactic)[/b][/h1]

[h2]After months of hard work, I’m pleased to present DUWS_T, packed with plenty of new content for players to explore[/h2]

Orig DUWS_X：https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2034171894&searchtext=DUWS

DUWS_T is a dynamic, persistent campaign generator that simulates full military operations from special ops to joint warfare. Developed from Tomata's DUWS_X, it includes numerous fixes and new features.

[h1][b]Features：[/b][/h1]
[h2]1.[/h2] The side mission system has been fully reworked. Time limits have been added to some missions, and mechanics have been revised. For instance, in the pilot rescue mission, the injured pilot will not follow you—you must carry him back to base on foot, or load him into a vehicle for transport. Different missions now grant different rewards: simple missions give modest rewards, while challenging ones will strengthen your army.
[h2]2.[/h2] 5 new missions added: CQB Clear the area, Armor Hunt, Secure Tactical Asset, Protect Informant, Recapture IDAP Truck.
[h2]3.[/h2] Support system fully reworked. All items have revised prices, with numerous quality-of-life improvements—such as HALO jumps now supporting AI-only insertions.
[h2]See more in the changelog...[/h2] https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/3685431219

This campaign encourages tactical thinking and maneuvering; one small mistake can lead to heavy losses for your army. Since much of DUWS_T has been revised, if you’re new to Arma 3 or haven’t played the DUWS series before, I recommend starting with the [b]default parameters[/b].

[h2]How To Play：[/h2]
If you want to learn as you play, open the map at the start of the mission—it contains the DUWS_T manual with all the basic info you need.

[h1][b]FAQ:[/b][/h1]

Does this mission require XXX mods? — No. As long as your PC isn’t a total potato, it’ll run just fine.

Can this mission be ported to other maps like DUWS_X? — Yes! Simply download the mission file and change the map name. LINK: https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3685431219/803468365964444679/

Can we play this on dedicated servers? — It should work. The mission fully supports single-player and LAN multiplayer, but hasn’t been tested on dedicated servers yet. If you run into bugs on servers, let me know.

Is this mission compatible with XXX mods? — It should work with most mods on the Steam Workshop, but compatibility with specific AI mods is for you to test. (Fully compatible with LAMBS and VCOM; others are unconfirmed.)

Can we play as XXX faction? — The mission includes XOR’s automatic faction detection system, which I’ve improved to work with nearly every properly configured faction mod on the Workshop, including factions built with ALiVE’s ORBAT Creator.

I’m a lazy a$$ — can I report bugs anywhere? — Doesn’t matter. I’ll take every bug report seriously.

Can I simulate Vietnam War or WWII in this mission? — Yes, but this mission was designed for modern warfare, so you might see things like modern stuff showing up in a WWII setting.
